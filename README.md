# Sales Performance Dashboard | Power BI

## Project Overview

This project presents an interactive **Sales Performance Dashboard** developed in **Power BI** to analyze business performance across sales, profitability, regions, and product categories. The dashboard enables stakeholders to monitor key metrics, identify trends, and make data-driven decisions.

## Dataset used
https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/ahmedmohamedibrahim1/sales-dataset

## Objectives

* Analyze overall sales and profitability.
* Track business performance over time.
* Identify high-performing regions and product categories.
* Provide actionable business insights through interactive visualizations.

## Dashboard Features

### KPI Cards

* Total Sales
* Total Profit
* Profit Margin %
* Total Orders
* Total Quantity Sold
* Average Discount

### Visualizations

* Annual Sales Trend Analysis
* Monthly Profit Trend Analysis
* Sales by Region
* Profit by Category
* Executive Business Insights

### Interactivity

* Region Filter
* Category Filter
* Dynamic Cross-Filtering

## Key Insights

* Generated **$2.30M** in total sales and **$286.4K** in profit.
* Achieved an overall **12.47% profit margin**.
* The **West region** emerged as the highest revenue contributor.
* **Technology** was the most profitable product category.
* **2017 recorded the strongest sales performance** across all years.
* Average discount of **15.62%** contributed to sales growth while impacting profitability.

## Presentation Summary

A companion PowerPoint presentation was created to communicate findings through a business storytelling approach:

**Sales Performance → Profit Performance → Regional Performance → Product Performance → Business Insights → Strategic Recommendations**

The presentation highlights key findings, strategic recommendations, and business opportunities derived from the dashboard analysis.
